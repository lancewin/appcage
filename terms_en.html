AppCage · October 6, 2026

What AppCage does

AppCage is a focus tool built on Apple's Screen Time framework: you tell it which apps or categories to restrict, and it writes those choices as system limits. When a restricted app is opened, iOS does the blocking — AppCage never touches that app's process.

Your responsibilities

  • You set and keep your own passcode and recovery code. The passcode is stored only in this device's Keychain and we cannot recover it for you; if you forget it, the recovery code is the only way to reset.
  • Please don't use this app for anything unlawful or harmful to others, and don't reverse engineer, crack or redistribute it.

Authorization and how limits take effect

The app depends on system authorization. Turning off Screen Time authorization in Settings lifts every limit at once; that switch is provided by iOS and keeping it on is your choice. How well limits work depends on your iOS version, authorization state and device state.

Data and privacy

The apps you restrict, your block history and your usage statistics all stay on this device. We neither collect nor upload them. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Trial and billing

Whether a free trial is offered, how many days it lasts and what each plan costs are all set by what the subscription page shows (provided by the App Store and subject to change in App Store Connect). A trial costs nothing; you're reminded one day before it ends, and it then renews at the plan you chose. A one-time purchase has no trial and is charged once.

Auto-renewal

The subscription renews automatically within 24 hours before the current period ends, charged to your Apple ID account. The renewal price matches the original purchase price; we may let you know before a price change.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancel any time in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Pro stays available until the end of the current period rather than switching off immediately. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies — we can't process them for you.

Restore purchases

After switching devices or reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases” on the subscription page with the same Apple ID to get Pro back at no extra cost.

What Pro includes

Pro unlocks:

  • Unlimited shield plans and app limits (the free version allows 1)
  • Scheduled plans, free time and temporary access
  • Full usage insights: day-by-day detail for about the last month
  • System limits: install and purchase restrictions, content and privacy ratings

About the free version

The free version works indefinitely, with basic shield plans and a limit of 1 item, without the Pro features above. We never switch off the plans you've already created just because you don't subscribe.
